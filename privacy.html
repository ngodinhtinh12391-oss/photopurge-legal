Privacy Policy

PhotoPurge · Effective 13 November 2026

PhotoPurge collects no data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Your photos stay on your device

You choose photos with the standard iOS photo picker, which gives PhotoPurge only the photos you select, not your whole library. PhotoPurge reads and removes their metadata on your iPhone. Photos and their metadata are never uploaded or sent to us or anyone else.

Temporary files

The cleaned copy is written to the app's temporary folder so you can share or save it. Temporary files are removed when you start a new photo and by iOS automatically.

Permissions

PhotoPurge asks for "Add Photos Only" access the first time you tap Save, so it can save the cleaned copy to your library. It cannot read your library with this permission. The map shown in the inspector uses the coordinates already inside your photo and does not use your current location.

Purchases

PhotoPurge Pro is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment details.

Children

PhotoPurge collects no information from anyone, including children.
