What does PhotoPurge remove?

GPS location, device make and model, lens and serial numbers, date and time, camera settings (aperture, shutter speed, ISO), and IPTC/XMP data. Image orientation is kept so your photo is not rotated.

Does it reduce photo quality?

No. When the format allows, PhotoPurge copies the image data without re-compressing it.

Why does the original still have location in Photos?

PhotoPurge never changes your original. It creates a clean copy that you share or save.

How do I restore PhotoPurge Pro?

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID.
